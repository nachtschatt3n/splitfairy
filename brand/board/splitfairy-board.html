The brief

A warm, mobile-first vacation planner for families who share meals, shopping and costs. It should feel fair, calm and trustworthy with money, with a light touch of magic, not like a spreadsheet or a banking app.

Concept A

Split Sparkle

A fairy's sparkle, cut into two identical halves.

  • Fairy and fair split in a single shape
  • The halves are exact 180° copies: nobody gets the bigger piece
  • Grows from the coral sparkle already in the app
  • Wordmark keeps the app's Outfit bold/light name, outlined
